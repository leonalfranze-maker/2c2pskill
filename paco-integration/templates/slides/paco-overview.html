04How it works

A payment in four steps

Your site

Customer clicks Pay

Your server asks PACO for a secure payment page

2C2P payment page

Customer pays

Chooses a method and pays, with OTP if the bank asks

PACO

Result is sent

PACO notifies your server and returns the customer to you

Your server

Confirm, then deliver

Check the final status with PACO before fulfilling the order

Golden rule: confirm before you deliver. A first "success" message or the customer's return to your site isn't proof of payment. Always check the status with PACO.

06Security

Security built in, end to end

Card data stays with 2C2P

With the payment page, card numbers never touch your systems, so your PCI DSS scope stays small.

Approved servers only

Only your allowlisted servers can connect to PACO.

Encrypted & signed

Production messages are signed and encrypted (JOSE) with keys you generate and keep.

Double-checked results

Every payment can be confirmed with PACO's status API before you deliver.
