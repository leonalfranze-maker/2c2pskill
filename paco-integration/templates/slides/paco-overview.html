What is PACO

One payment API for your whole checkout

PACO is 2C2P's payment platform. Your systems connect to it once, and your customers can pay with cards, wallets, QR and more, on a secure 2C2P payment page.

How it works

A payment in four steps

1
Your site
Customer clicks Pay. Your server asks PACO for a payment page.
2
2C2P page
Customer chooses a method and pays. OTP if the bank asks.
3
PACO
Tells your server the result and sends the customer back to you.
4
Your server
Confirms the final status with PACO, then fulfils the order.
Always confirm the status with PACO before delivering. A first "success" message or the customer's return to your site isn't proof of payment.

PACO overview
Security

Security built in

🛡️ Card data stays with 2C2P

With the payment page, card numbers never touch your systems, which keeps your PCI DSS scope small.

🔐 Encrypted and signed

Production messages are signed and encrypted (JOSE), with keys you generate and keep.

🌐 Approved servers only

Only your allowlisted servers can connect to PACO.

✅ Double-checked results

Every payment can be confirmed with PACO's status API before you deliver.
