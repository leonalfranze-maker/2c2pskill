03The solution

What 2C2P PACO gives us

One integration, many payment methods

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, JCB, UnionPay…), wallets, QR, internet banking, over-the-counter and installments, as enabled on our account.

Secure hosted payment page

2C2P's mobile-friendly page collects card details and runs the bank's OTP check (3-D Secure). Card numbers never reach our systems.

The full payment lifecycle by API

Create payments, check their status, cancel (void), capture (settle) and refund, all from our own systems.

Sandbox and Merchant Portal

A test environment with test cards to build safely, and a portal to view transactions, handle refunds and manage users.

06Security & compliance

Built to keep money and card data safe

Card numbers never touch our servers. The 2C2P page collects them, keeping our PCI DSS scope small.
Encrypted and signed messages in production (JOSE), with keys we generate and keep.
Only our approved servers can connect (IP allowlist).
Credentials stay in secure storage, never in code or chat.
Every payment is double-checked with PACO's status API before we deliver.

Our requirements

Internal security review, data-protection or audit requirements, PCI DSS status, approvals needed before launch.
